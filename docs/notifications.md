# Notifications

In Mail, Calendar, or Drive, open the account menu and choose **Notification
settings**. Select **Enable notifications**, then allow the browser's permission
request. Use **Send test** to check the connection; choose **Disable
notifications** to turn it off on that device. Browser or operating system
settings can also block notifications. Enable each product and device separately.
The mail privacy control chooses whether mail notifications may include sender
and subject; by default they show only “New message”.

On iPhone and iPad, Web Push requires iOS or iPadOS 16.4 or later and an installed
web app. In Safari, use Share → Add to Home Screen, open the app from the Home
Screen, and enable notifications there. Delivery depends on browser and device
notification settings. New-mail and calendar-reminder triggers are added
separately from this foundation.

Mail may offer notifications on first installed launch or after three browser
sessions. **Later** and the dismiss button postpone the suggestion for 14 days;
it is shown at most three times per origin on this device. The settings switch
remains available. A denied permission must be changed in browser or device
settings.
