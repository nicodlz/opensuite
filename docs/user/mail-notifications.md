# Mail notifications

Enable notifications for Mail in the application's notification settings and allow notifications in your browser. New unread messages delivered to Inbox can then produce a notification even when the app is in the background. Clicking a notification opens its conversation. A group of new messages may produce one notification that opens the Inbox.

The privacy setting controls whether notifications show the sender and subject. With details off, notifications say “New message”. The message body is never included. Older mail, messages in Junk, and messages moved to Inbox do not normally produce notifications. Browser and push service availability affect delivery.
